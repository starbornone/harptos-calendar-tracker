# harptos-calendar-tracker

Harptos Calendar Tracker is a Dungeon Master tool for Dungeons & Dragons campaigns. The Vite and TypeScript app uses shadcn/ui. Set or advance the Harptos clock, browse its current month and festivals, switch campaign locations, and see local light, clock markers, and hourly weather. Use the copy icon beside the one-line campaign summary to copy it into your notes. Use **Share Campaign** to send a link to the current moment. Recipients can preview it and choose whether to adopt it as their saved campaign. Your browser saves the time, location, and weather seed. A new campaign starts in Neverwinter at **08:00 on 1 Tarsakh 1491 DR, Year of the Scarlet Witch**.

The selector offers the five Harptos locations: Neverwinter, Phandalin, Baldur’s Gate, Waterdeep, and Halruaa. Locations using other calendars are hidden for now; existing saves and shared links still open with their original calendar.

The tracker defaults to English. Browsers with a saved Simplified Chinese preference still use it for the interface, campaign summary, and footer disclaimer.

Open the [Harptos Calendar Tracker](https://harptos.astralarchives.games/) or follow the local setup below.

The homepage footer links to companion tools: Chronicler, D&D Point-Buy Calculator, World Binder, Open Tome, and Pathfinder 2e Calculator.
