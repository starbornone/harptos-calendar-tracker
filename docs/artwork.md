# Artwork

[Sword Coast background](../public/art/sword-coast.webp) is an original image generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then encoded as WebP for the tracker. It is a static decorative backdrop for the header and campaign clock, ending before the calendar.

The style references were the local homepage assets from Chronicler (`D:/Dev/chronicler/public/kingmaker-trading-post.webp`) and World Binder (`D:/Dev/world-binder/apps/web/public/art/bellwatch.webp`). Those images were supplied as style references rather than copied into this project.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background illustration for the upper header and clock section of a Forgotten Realms Harptos calendar web app. Primary request: an original atmospheric Sword Coast fantasy landscape matching the painterly realism, textured natural materials, deep shadow, misty layered hills and warm amber lanterns of the two supplied reference images. Input images: image 1 (Chronicler trading post) and image 2 (World Binder Bellwatch) are style references only, not edit targets. Scene: a northern coastal river city inspired by Neverwinter, viewed from a wooded hillside at blue hour, distant stone walls, a few medieval towers and slate roofs, a stone bridge over water, evergreen hills disappearing into mist, faint warm light on the horizon. Composition: very wide landscape 3:2 or wider, distant city and a warmly lit tower in the right third, much of the left and central region is quiet dark forest and mist suitable for overlaid cream text and clock controls. Make the painting beautiful and legible as a web backdrop. Palette: charcoal, muted pine green, slate blue, warm ochre and small amber highlights. Style: richly painted fantasy book landscape, subtle visible brushwork, atmospheric depth, grounded medieval architecture, restrained drama. No close characters, no lettering, no typography, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no decorative frame. This is a static atmospheric scene, not an illustration of a clock.
